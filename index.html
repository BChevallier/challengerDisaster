73 seconds.

$1.7 billion.

7 lives.

73 s: liftoff 11:38 a.m. EST, 28 Jan 1986; the flight "ended 73 seconds later" (Rogers Vol. 1, p. 19; structural breakup at 73.137 s, p. 21).

$1.7 billion: what Endeavour, the orbiter built to replace Challenger, cost (NASA, Space Shuttle Era Facts). Orbiter only, partly built from spares; not Challenger's own price.

7: Scobee, Smith, Onizuka, Resnik, McNair, McAuliffe, Jarvis.

  • Solid-fuel boosters, never used before to fly people

Design chosen Mar 1972; solid boosters chosen mainly for lower development cost (Rogers pp. 2–4).

Thiokol design based on the Titan III, with two O-rings, a longer tang, reusable segments (pp. 120–122).
